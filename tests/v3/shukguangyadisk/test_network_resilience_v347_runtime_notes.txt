v3.4.7 network resilience expectations:
- transient DNS/connection failures do not invalidate login tokens;
- requests retry 3 times total, then enter a short per-host circuit breaker;
- repeated outage warnings are throttled;
- account summary uses recent successful cache during transient outages;
- folder scans mark incomplete inventory truncated and retry later;
- network recovery resumes automatic monitoring without a MoviePilot restart.
